# Analysis: Socratic Wisdom in the Age of AI, ChatGPT vs. Human Tutors (Fakour & Imani, 2025)

*Superseded by [`../evidence-base.md`](../evidence-base.md). Historical record only; see Part 1 there, these are perceptions, not learning outcomes.*

An empirical study of 230 students contrasting AI and human Socratic tutors.

## Findings

* **AI's structural advantage is the non-judgmental, self-paced safe space.** ChatGPT
  scored highest of all measured factors on "comfortable asking questions" (4.9) and "learn
  at my own pace" (4.8), driven by 24/7 access and freedom from fear of judgment.

* **Trust in accuracy is AI's biggest weakness, 3.7 vs. 4.5 for humans.** Students rated
  AI markedly lower on trusting the accuracy of information, reflecting fear of
  plausible-sounding but wrong responses.

* **Humans win on tailored follow-up and depth, not on availability.** Human tutors led on
  personalized feedback (4.6 vs. 3.8), engagement (4.7 vs. 3.9), and helping understand
  complex concepts (4.8 vs. 4.0). Their edge is dynamically adapting questioning to what the
  learner just said.

* **Unstructured Socratic dialogue degenerates.** Without skilled facilitation, open-ended Socratic
  discussion risks digressions and shallow engagement; structure plus reflection is what
  produces gains.

* **A hybrid model is the recommended target, not pure AI.** The MLP analysis shows
  polarized preferences; the authors conclude the optimal design blends AI's accessibility
  and safe space with human-style tailored, emotionally-aware facilitation.
